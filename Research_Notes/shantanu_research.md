# Feasibility Analysis: Fog-Clearing Device for Intersections

## 1. Fluid Dynamics & Aerodynamics ke Flaws
* **Open System Constraint:** Chouraha ek unconfined space hai. Jaise hi fan thodi hawa clear karega, surrounding wind turant naya fog waha push kar degi. Ek open system me volume infinite hota hai.
* **Short-Circuiting of Airflow:** Fan sirf apne aas-paas ka fog khichega. Pura chouraha clear karne ke liye jo suction force (CFM) chahiye, wo ek industrial jet engine se hi aayega, chote fan se nahi.
* **Pressure Drop Issue:** Fan ke aage ESP (High voltage) plates aur acoustic chamber hone se airflow me itna massive pressure drop aayega ki fan hawa ko theek se suck hi nahi kar payega.
* **Boundary Layer Turbulence:** Traffic aur gaadiyo ki wajah se waha ki hawa continuously turbulent rehti hai, jisse device ka intake system fail ho jayega.

## 2. Acoustic Chamber (Acoustic Agglomeration) ke Flaws
* **Extreme Noise Pollution:** Fog particles (micron size) ko vibrate karke jodne ke liye high-decibel (130dB+) ya ultrasonic sound waves chahiye. Chourahe par ye sound logo ke kaan faad dega ya severe aural issues karega.
* **Residence Time Missing:** Particles ko agglomerate (bada) hone ke liye chamber me kuch seconds rukna padega. Par intake fan ki speed se hawa itni jaldi niklegi ki particles ko judne ka time hi nahi milega.
* **Resonance Failure:** Ek open-ended flow duct me standing acoustic waves banana bohot mushkil hai, sound energy bina kisi kaam ke dissipate ho jayegi.

## 3. High Voltage Drop (Electrostatic Precipitator - ESP) ke Flaws
* **Short Circuit / Electrocution Risk:** Heavy moisture (fog) aur High Voltage (corona discharge) ka combo public place par bohot dangerous hai. Arc banega aur machine short-circuit ho jayegi.
* **Ozone Gas Generation:** High voltage jab hawa ko ionize karta hai, tab by-product me Ozone (O3) banti hai. Chourahe par khade logo ko saans lene me dikkat aur choking hogi.
* **Wet Operation Failure:** ESPs dry dust (chimney smoke) ke liye hote hain. Fog me water droplets electrodes par chipak kar spark-over kar denge.

## 4. Thermodynamics & Mechanical Collection ke Flaws
* **Extremely Low Water Content (LWC):** Fog me pani bohot kam hota hai (sirf 0.05 se 0.5 gram per cubic meter). Hazaaro liter hawa khichne ke baad bhi sirf 1-2 bund pani collect hoga. Mechanical collection unit lagana useless hai.
* **Freezing & Blocking Risk:** Srinagar aur baki jagaho par fog ke time temperature zero ke aas paas hota hai. Jo thoda bohot pani collect hoga, wo mechanical unit me freeze hokar pipe aur mesh ko block kar dega.
* **High Blockage from Exhaust:** Traffic wale chourahe par fog ke sath gaadiyo ka dhua (carbon soot) bhi fan me jayega. Ye soot ESP plates aur water collector ko ek hi din me completely block kar dega.

## 5. Manufacturing & Prototyping ke Flaws
* **Testing Challenge:** Isko test kaha karoge? Wait karoge fog aane ka? Artificial fog room / environmental chamber banana hi apne aap me ek alag B.Tech project hai.
* **Cost & Procurement:** High-capacity blowers, high-voltage corona transformers, aur industrial acoustic transducers college ke project budget se bahar jayenge.
* **Interdisciplinary Complexity:** Tum log Mech Engg me ho, par isme High-voltage electronics aur complex acoustic physics heavily involve hai. Ye 3rd year ke ek semester me properly code/wire karna feasible nahi hai.
* **Massive Power Consumption:** In teeno components (Blower + ESP + Acoustics) ko chalane ke liye itni electricity chahiye ki normal street-light pole ka connection overload ho jayega.
* **Installation & Mounting:** Itne heavy aur bulky unit ko chourahe ke pole par vertically mount karna structural hazard hai, aur zameen par rakha toh traffic obstruct karega.
* **Efficiency vs ROI:** Is device ko chalane ki electrical cost aur maintenance cost itni zyada hogi ki uske comparison me "2 meter extra visibility" bilkul justify nahi hoti.
* **Visual Obstruction:** Visibility clear karne ke chakkar me tumhara bada sa device khud chourahe ke centre me ek visual blind-spot create kar dega, jisse accidents ho sakte hain.
